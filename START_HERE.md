+## 🎨 Customization

+

+### Update Products

+Edit `components/Products.tsx` (lines 8-51) to modify product information.

+

+### Change Colors

+Modify `tailwind.config.ts` to customize the gold and noir color palettes.

+

+### Replace Images

+Update Unsplash URLs in components with your own product images.

+

+### Modify Content

+Edit text directly in component files (Hero.tsx, About.tsx, etc.).
